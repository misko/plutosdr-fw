# Issue 107: counter-to-UTC implementation and qualification ledger

Status: **software-tested source candidate; not hardware-qualified**.

The source change adds a sideband SCANTIME command to the adaptive iiOD
provider. It reports a session/generation-bound coherent counter, boot UUID,
provider epoch and device monotonic read interval. The full counter epoch is
accepted only after DMA rebasing. Queries share the provider's ownership and
scheduler locks, return EAGAIN on contention, and cannot fault acquisition on
read failure. Existing IQ/visit/terminal records are unchanged.

The host brackets queries, retains chrony UTC-error evidence and checks rate,
epoch, counter progression, anchor coverage and clock steps. The explicit
policy is <=50 ms full query width and <=+/-100 ms absolute error across the
whole capture, including oscillator drift and acquisition alignment.

Current kernel snapshots attest coherence, **not snapshot age**. The daemon
therefore reports unknown age. Host qualification requires independently
validated hardware bounds identified by report, radio serial and boot UUID.
No default calibration is supplied. Reboot invalidates the boot-bound profile.
This prevents a low RTT or stable realtime/monotonic offset from being promoted
to an unsupported UTC-accuracy claim.

The tracker imports new evidence into IQ manifest major 6; old persisted
contracts remain unchanged. Its recording reader independently replays the
qualified origin/full-span error bound. The temporary legacy two-second gate
cannot override an unqualified counter-timing record. For qualified captures,
TLE matching tests a shared UTC offset on a <=25 ms grid with a frozen
training/held-out split, retaining existing orbital-tau, polynomial-null and
wrong-time diagnostics. Grid instability causes abstention. The grid is a
diagnostic, not proof of stability at all real-valued offsets.

## Remaining bounded hardware gate

The test radio and independently UTC-timed RF marker source must first be
identified. Calibration and acceptance data must be separate. Record exact
radio/boot/firmware identity and instrument, cable and marker-detection error.

1. Verify counter continuity and UTC markers in one 300-second capture at each
   of 10, 15 and 20 MS/s (15 minutes total).
2. Spend no more than ten additional capture minutes on loaded transport,
   reboot, unavailable/unlocked/stale UTC and clock-step cases. Successful
   captures must retain truthful unqualified timing when a bound is unavailable.
3. Require the first/last marker within ten seconds of the capture endpoints
   and marker gaps no greater than ten seconds. Every absolute residual plus
   reference uncertainty must fit the reported full-capture error bound.
4. Run `python -m pluto_plus.counter_utc_verify manifest.json reference.json`;
   retain its input hashes, residuals and verdict together with continuity and
   scan-scheduling evidence.
5. Measure tracking wall time (up to nine fits/group) and candidate sensitivity
   on the existing corpus. Do not turn finite-grid stability into identity.

Only after this gate passes should a reviewed calibration profile be installed
and the source candidate promoted to a hardware-qualified release. Deployment
and a hardware-accuracy claim remain pending, not implicitly approved by the
software test results.

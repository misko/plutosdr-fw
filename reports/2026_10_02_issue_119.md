persistent deployment to radio `1040007c4a94000211000b009186843ef2` at

## Scan deadline compatibility

The initial kernel-only v0.59 RC1 passed the tone test but intermittently failed
a 5-second, 2.5 MS/s slow-attack scan with terminal reason 6 / `-ETIME`:
39 planned, 37 delivered, 2 cancelled. The stronger attestation makes the
measured completion later. iiOD could select a last dwell that fitted nominally,
then fail the entire session when the actual post-recall boundary no longer
fitted before its end. Five full matrices on the released v0.59 image passed;
that does not establish immunity to the timing edge case.

RC2 fixes the interaction: policy commit distinguishes deadline exhaustion from
backward time. iiOD reserves its queue window only once the measured dwell fits.
A late final selection is excluded from the ledger, earlier windows remain
drainable, and normal scan completion restores the LO. A session that cannot
capture even its first dwell still fails closed. Stop uses at least the latest
observed counter. Hardware/SPI errors and malformed timing still fail closed.

A deterministic test keeps one completed visit undrained, injects a successful
late recall, and checks normal completion, delivery, no cancellation, and
restoration. It fails against unmodified v0.59 and passes with the fix. Additional
policy tests distinguish backward time, over-deadline time and an exact fit.

## Build and validation

The kernel configuration matches the extracted v0.59 configuration byte for
byte. The packaged rootfs retains all 767 archive entries; only `opt/VERSIONS`,
`usr/sbin/iiod`, and `usr/lib/libiio.so.0.25` change. The FPGA and device-tree
payloads are reused from the hash-verified v0.59 image. Reproduction scripts are
under `scripts/issue119/`.

All 46 production-function driver fault tests pass. The ARM kernel and iiOD
builds pass. Six native scheduler/radio/protocol/queue test executables pass,
including 24,000 queue boundary cases. The firmware source-routing suite has
240 passing tests. Hardware qualification and deployment results follow below.

The remaining limitation is explicit: a matching selector, profile SRAM and
asserted PLL lock do not independently measure physical frequency on every
production visit. RF loopback is separate end-to-end qualification. This change
preserves the Fast Lock approach selected by the user.

  recalls; all 8 tone/channel cells passed. Counter brackets were 6,332–7,512

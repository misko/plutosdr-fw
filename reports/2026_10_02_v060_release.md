## Validation

The combined source passes seven native policy/radio/protocol/session/queue/
capacity/time executables, including both issues' regression cases, 24,000
queue boundaries and 256 activity masks. The session passes ASan/UBSan.
The driver passes all 46 production-function fault tests. ARM iiOD builds pass,
27 release-routing/tooling tests pass, and the immutable remote source graph
passes. The unchanged PPU changes passed their CI matrix before merge.

Before flash, the integrated daemon passed native 1.25 and 2.5 MS/s captures,
DMA fault injection and cancellation on `.15`. The exact kernel had separately
passed the #119 RF loopback and scan matrix on `.18`; that earlier evidence is
in `reports/issue119/v059`. No new TX or loopback test was performed on `.15`.

PPU verified the physical flash boundary, protected regions, written FIT,
software reboot, returned serial/version, SSH key rotation and disabled TX.
Receipt: `a10973d0-cbc8-4700-921d-71a5a9b70864`.

Successful cells have zero skipped, invalid or cancelled visits. Native IQ is
150,000 samples per receiver per 120 ms low-rate visit; 88.8 MB of manual
low-rate IQ was retained. Legacy v2 at 1.25 MS/s delivered 74/74. Injected DMA
failure returned a restored EIO terminal in 3.32 seconds; cancellation after
three records restored settings in 4.38 seconds. Every post-boot attempt,
including failures, restored receiver settings and allowed an ordinary
32,768-byte dual-RX capture afterward.

## Known limitations

The first 10 MS/s manual cell failed with terminal reason 3 / `-ETIMEDOUT`
after 38 delivered visits; two remaining records were explicitly cancelled.
Five subsequent 10 MS/s cells passed (four manual, one slow attack). The exact
cause of the intermittent recall timeout is unresolved. The driver retains
its checked two-millisecond lock-poll deadline; no timing guard or acceptance
criterion was weakened. This release does not claim an uninterrupted 10 MS/s
pass across all attempts. Both the failure and follow-ups are retained.

Use 500k transport blocks at 1.25 MS/s; the earlier #120 qualification showed
capacity skips with 1M blocks. Fast Lock selector/SRAM/lock attestation does
not independently measure physical RF frequency on every production visit.
Kernel snapshot-age remains unknown. Software reboot is verified; physical
power cycling is untested. Leo archive/import support and the 25/75 production
policy remain outside this release's qualification.
